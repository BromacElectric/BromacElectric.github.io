BROMAC ELECTRIC INC.

COMMERCIAL ELECTRICAL CONSTRUCTION

Family owned and operated since 2019, Bromac Electric is backed by 46 years of electrical experience. We combine decades of trusted industry expertise with top-tier electrical solutions to deliver dependable results for our clients.

We are solution providers who take pride in seeing projects through to completion, from the initial planning stages to the final details. We value the long-standing relationships we have built with our business partners and project owners and understand that successful projects are built on trust, communication and a commitment to doing things right.

At Bromac Electric, we are invested in providing alternative construction options and are always looking for practical, cost-effective strategies that benefit our clients and owner groups. By combining experience with a solutions-focused approach, we work to deliver quality electrical services while helping our clients make informed decisions throughout every stage of their project.
